about us

Handmade. Made with love.

ITZGA wasn’t born in a factory. It was born in a kitchen, with a pot, a few molds and the idea of doing things right, without rushing.

We wanted something beautiful that smelled wonderful, but above all, something honest. Candles that don’t fill your home with black smoke. Soaps that don’t dry out your skin. Products you can gift with pride, because you know what’s inside.

So we started testing. Candles that melted crooked, soaps that looked like jelly, scents that wouldn’t hold. And between one mistake and the next, we found what we were looking for: the joy of making it by hand.

At ITZGA everything is made in small batches, right here in Mexico City. We only use soy wax, vegetable glycerin, shea butter, oats, clays and essential oils. Ingredients your skin recognizes. Nothing more.

Every bear, every donut, every tricolor bar and every vanilla candle is unmolded and cut by hand, one by one. That’s why no two pieces are identical. That’s why each one smells and feels different.

We’re not a mass-market brand. We’re a small workshop that believes handmade isn’t a trend, it’s a return to the essentials. It’s lighting a candle and feeling like you’ve come home.

Thank you for making a little piece of ours a part of your home.
