Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before placing your order

How do I place an order?

Add your products with the “Add to order” button, open the cart 🛍 and send it by WhatsApp or email. You can also write to us from the contact page. We’ll confirm prices and availability.

Where are you based and where do you deliver?

We’re an artisan workshop in Mexico City. Write to us with your postal code and we’ll confirm whether we reach your area, plus costs and delivery times.

How much do the products cost?

The price depends on the product and quantity. Write to us on WhatsApp or by email and we’ll share it right away.

What payment methods do you accept?

When we confirm your order, we’ll tell you the payment methods available.

How long does my order take?

Everything is handmade in small batches, so the time depends on what you order and what we have in stock. We’ll give you an estimated date when we confirm.

Do you make custom orders or event favors?

Write to us with what you have in mind (quantity, date, colors or scents) and we’ll let you know if we can make it.

What ingredients do you use?

We work with ingredients such as soy wax, vegetable glycerin, shea butter, oats, clays and essential oils. In the catalog, each soap lists its main ingredients. If you have allergies, write to us before ordering and we’ll confirm the full ingredients of the piece.

Are the soaps suitable for sensitive skin?

Every skin is different. If you have sensitive skin or allergies, write to us and we’ll help you choose. We recommend testing on a small area first.

How do I take care of my handmade soap?

Let it dry between uses on a draining soap dish, away from running water. That way it lasts longer.

How do I use my candle safely?

Light it on a stable, heat-resistant surface, away from curtains and flammable objects. Never leave it unattended or within reach of children or pets. Trim the wick to about 5 mm (1/4 inch) before each use and put it out if the flame is too high or there is a lot of smoke.

Are the edible candles meant to be eaten?

Edible candles are made with kitchen ingredients, such as butter or chocolate, to be tasted. Ask us about the ingredients and allergens of each piece, store them in a cool place and don’t eat the wick.

Are all pieces identical?

No. Each piece is unmolded and cut by hand, so color, shape or scent may vary slightly from one to the next.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Write to us as soon as possible with photos of the product and the packaging so we can look into your case.
